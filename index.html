About Us

Gifted Eateries and Restaurant is a premier food manufacturing and catering business based in the heart of Ikene, Ogun State. We specialize in delivering mouth-watering Nigerian cuisine and comprehensive catering services for weddings, corporate events, parties, and everyday dining. Our commitment to quality ingredients, hygienic food preparation, and timely delivery has made us the first choice for discerning clients across Ogun State. Whether you need meals for 20 or 2,000 guests, our experienced team ensures every dish meets the highest standards of taste and presentation.

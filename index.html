About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Oilfield Equipment and Drilling Solutions

Brigail Oilfield Equipment Limited (also trading as Brigail Drilling Tools Co) is a Nigerian-owned enterprise specializing in the supply of high-quality oilfield equipment and drilling tools to the energy sector. Led by experienced energy sector consultants, we understand the critical demands of oil and gas operations and provide equipment solutions that meet international standards.

Based in Ikene, Ogun State, we serve clients across Nigeria's oil-producing regions with integrity, technical expertise, and unwavering commitment to safety and quality. Our mission is to be the preferred supplier of oilfield equipment by delivering reliable products, expert technical support, and exceptional service that keeps Nigeria's energy operations running smoothly.
